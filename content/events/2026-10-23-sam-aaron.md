## About

An open meetup of Rotterdam’s live coding community in the WegGeefWinkel! 

This time with special guest: [Sam Aaron](https://sam.aaron.name/), creator of [Sonic Pi](https://sonic-pi.net/index.html).

The participating artists will show their work environments and creative ideas. The event is an informal yet informative open session anyone from the public is very welcome to take part, you do not need to have an experience in live coding or software development. The idea is to share, learn and get inspired.

The Rotterdam Live Coders community is a dynamic group that welcomes anyone with curiosity and a will to participate. The session will be hosted by Rotterdam artists Niki Scheijen (Nikilia), Rik Mertens (trem_werk), Seb Pappalardo (eerie_ear), Den Ree (den_ree).

As ever, if time permits, there will be a "from scratch" jam session at the end.
