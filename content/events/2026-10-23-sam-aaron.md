## About

An open meetup of Rotterdam’s live coding community in the WegGeefWinkel! 

The Rotterdam Live Coders Community meetup series, in which we gather to share our creative artistic processes, is organizing a special event. UK based artist-developer [Sam Aaron](https://sam.aaron.name/) will be in Rotterdam. At WGW we will organize an open and free event. It will be a combination of a walkthrough presentation of SuperSonic, the powerhouse in [Sonic Pi](https://sonic-pi.net/index.html), his live coding environment followed by an open stage live coding jam at the end.
