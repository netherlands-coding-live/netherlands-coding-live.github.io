## De Computerkamer @ DIY Festival - Nacht van Strijp-S

Live Coding Eindhoven will host this workshop paired with an Algorave in the evening at DIY Festival - Nacht van Strijp-S 2026! Both workshop and Algorave will take place in The Klokgebouw, where through a collaboration of multiple artists, one of its rooms will be transformed into "De Computerkamer" (the Computer Room). 

## About DIY Festival

DIY Festival is the festival for local creators, artists, and anyone looking to kickstart their career in the event or cultural sector. While the doors of major venues often remain closed to emerging artists, we open them wide. Here, young talents get the chance to showcase their creativity and show the city what they have to offer.

DIY Festival starts at 16:00 and ends at 05:00

The festival is part of Nacht van Strijp-S.

## About Nacht van Strijp-S

De Nacht van Strijp-S is a 24-hour festival where you can immerse yourself in a world of music, culture, food & drink, movement, and shopping. Supported by entrepreneurs, residents, institutions, and volunteers based at Strijp-S.

Nacht van Strijp-S starts at 19 September and ends at 20 September 2026.
