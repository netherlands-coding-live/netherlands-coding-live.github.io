## About

The deadline for the [ICLC (International Conference on Live Coding) 2027](https://iclc.toplap.org/2027/) in Antwerp is coming closer (1st of September).

We organize this meetup to get together at the end of the summer and discuss our proposals for the ICLC. If you are planning to submit a proposal and still have some questions we can discuss those during the meetup as well! A proposal can be for a performance, workshop, installation or paper. 

During the meetup we can share thoughts/ideas, give feedback on proposals, see what is still missing or unclear. We can also look at how to format the proposal, using the submission templates from the repository https://codeberg.org/iclc/iclc-templates/src/branch/main/2027, and how the submission website works.
