## About  
💻✨ Monthly Eindhoven Coding Live Meet-Up at BlaBlaLab ✨💻

Got a work-in-progress, a new tool to share, or an idea you'd like to explore with fellow makers?

Every last Tuesday of the month, BlaBlaLab opens up for Eindhoven's live coding community: creative coders, generative visualists, algorithmic sound-makers and the simply curious, all in one room.

Expect a Share Your Screen moment where anyone can show what they've been building (finished, messy, or half-broken, all welcome), a group brainstorm on an ongoing community project, and a jam session where laptops, instruments and curiosity collide.

Hosted by Wilbert & Erwin.

🕡 18:30 – 21:30
📍 Baltan Laboratories, Kastanjelaan 500, Eindhoven
🎟️ No registration required, just show up

Bring your laptop, your curiosity, and your unfinished ideas. No experience required: everyone is welcome!

Hosted at Baltan Laboratories, BlaBlaLab, Eindhoven
