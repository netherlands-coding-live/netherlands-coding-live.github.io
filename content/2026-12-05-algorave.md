## About

What grows when a community of live coders comes to the surface?


Fruiting Bodies is an algorave: a night of music and visuals made live from code. The code is projected on the screen, so you can watch the sound and images being written in real time while you dance to them.

Like the mushrooms on our poster, a fruiting body is only the visible part of something much larger, a network growing out of sight. This evening, the live coding community of BlaBlaLab comes to the surface, with performers from the community taking over the Paviljoen, joined by special international guest Bruno Gola.
